- **WhatsApp photo viewer stays inside the WhatsApp view.** It was laid out against the whole window but painted inside the workspace, so the backdrop and photo slid under the Floq sidebar and the arrows sat on top of it; large photos also spilled out because the image height was not bounded. The viewer now covers exactly the WhatsApp view and any photo fits it, centred; zoom stays clipped.
