# Floq v0.6.28

## Changes

- **Canvas: cross-project wire chips.** The chips at the top right of the canvas, which show agents wired to agents in other projects, now fade out on their own after 6 seconds. Hovering a chip pauses its timer and shows an X that closes it right away. A closed chip stays hidden until the app restarts, the X can be reached with the keyboard, and with reduced motion on, chips disappear without fading.

## Not in this release

- **Memory screen graph redesign.** The new graph view (smoother rendering, grouped view, shared controls) is still in progress and will ship in a later release.

This is a pre-release.
